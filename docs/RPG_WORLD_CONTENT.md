# Taskbar Hero RPG/MMORPG World Content

## World hierarchy

World > Biome > Sub-biome > Zone > Sub-zone > Point of Interest > Instance.

## Biomes

Greenwilds, Sun Plains, Ashen Desert, Frostcrown, Murkfen, Emberreach, Storm Coast, Aethermoor and Voidscar.

Each biome contains multiple sub-biomes with distinct encounters, resources, weather, traversal and enemy tables.

## World zones

Nine progression bands cover levels 1-100. Zones include frontier, plains, desert, frozen, swamp, volcanic, storm, arcane and void environments. Each zone contains four named sub-zones, plus towns, camps, resource nodes, portals, dungeons and event locations.

## Trails

Five repeatable traversal/combat trails provide checkpoint progression and cache rewards. Trails can later support timed leaderboards and seasonal records.

## Dungeons

Seven instanced dungeons range from normal to mythic difficulty. Mechanics include poison/root, flooding, mirrors, freezing, lava, gravity, spell reflection and time/reality effects.

## Raids

Three multi-wing raids support 8-20 players, weekly lockouts and multiple bosses. Raid encounters are designed around tank/healer/DPS/support party roles.

## Tower of Ascension

100 floors are generated as structured progression content. Every 10th floor is a boss floor, every 5th floor is an elite floor, and other floors rotate combat, traversal and wave objectives. Floors scale from level 1 through 100 and change environmental theme by ten-floor tier.

## RPG itemization

Weapons include swords, greatswords, bows, staves and relic blades. Armor supports heavy, medium and cloth archetypes. Equipment includes rings, relics, shields and off-hand items. Rarity tiers are common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary and mythic.

## Inventory

The baseline inventory model contains 60 bag slots and 200 bank slots. Stackable categories include materials, consumables, quest items and currencies. Equipment slots include head, neck, shoulders, chest, back, hands, waist, legs, feet, main hand, off hand, rings and relic.

## Crafting

Blacksmithing, Armoring, Leatherworking, Alchemy, Enchanting and Jewelcrafting provide production paths for weapons, armor, consumables, gems, enchants and relics.

## MMO systems

Character progression, combat, quests, parties, guilds, friends, mail, chat, trading, marketplace, auction house, achievements, reputations, titles, research, masteries, housing, pets, mounts and seasonal content are represented in the system registry.

Authoritative state such as loot, currency, inventory, combat results and trading must be validated and persisted server-side for an actual MMO deployment.
